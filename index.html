📈 Auto schedule requirement

Inbound agents & new staff wanted on each shift. Auto schedule fills these first; anyone left over goes to the shift that's least filled. Off days are spread so each day can meet its total. Public holidays use the Sunday row.
Used by ⚡ Auto schedule to decide how many inbound agents go on M / MD / E each day. These are agents actually on calls (after breaks) for 80% of calls answered in 30s. Pre-filled from your CC Dashboard traffic (Oct–Nov, busy-day volume, 288s handle time). Public holidays use the Sunday row.

🏖️ AL quota — per week & per day

Limit how much Annual Leave inbound agents and new staff can apply for — per week (Sun–Sat) and/or per day. Approved AL and pending AL requests both count; a half day counts as 0.5. Once a week is full, agents can't submit more AL for it — TLs and managers can still add AL directly (you'll get a warning). Leave the limit empty for no limit.
Weeks — set a different limit for a busy or quiet week
Days — open or limit a specific day (e.g. Christmas Eve: 2)
